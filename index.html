02 / About

        
A little about me

      

      

        

          
Hey! I'm AJ, a Computer Science and Business Administration student at USC (class of 2027) in Los Angeles. I'm happiest when I'm building: a weekend hackathon, a side project that gets out of hand, or a tool that a real business ends up using every day.

          
Lately I've been digging into how LLMs work under the hood. I wrote my own inference engine to find out, and I've been building AI systems that hold up in production, not just in a demo. I care a lot about the unglamorous parts too: tests for the edge cases, pipelines you can safely rerun, and security you never have to think about.

          
The business half of my degree shapes how I build. I like solving problems people actually have, and I'm always looking for the next big thing to build. If you're working on something exciting, I'd love to hear about it.
